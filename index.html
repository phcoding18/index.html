PAYROLL INFOGRAPHIC SUMMARY

Gross Pay

Total income an employee earns before deductions.

  • Daily: Rate × Days Worked
  • Monthly: Fixed Monthly Rate
  • Part-time: Hourly Rate × Hours Worked

Deductions

Amounts subtracted from gross pay:

  • Part-time: Only Withholding Tax = 10% of Gross
  • Full-time (Daily/Monthly):
    • SSS = 4% of Gross
    • PhilHealth = 2% of Gross
    • Pag-IBIG = ₱100 fixed
    • Withholding Tax = 10% of (Gross - SSS - PhilHealth - Pag-IBIG)

Net Pay

Amount the employee receives after all deductions.

Net = Gross - Total Deductions
